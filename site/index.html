Compliance, product safety and quality, on every record

Licensed cannabis is held to food and drug standards. Hendrix turns those standards into the daily work your team already does, so the proof builds itself as you go.

Compliance

Meet your state's rules and be able to show it on any day an inspector walks in.

  • Your state's rulebook loaded from the official PDF, organized by topic and linked to the records that meet each rule
  • METRC tags on every lot and package. METRC Check compares Hendrix with your METRC export: lab status, holds, shipments and counts
  • The 21 CFR 117 checklist food-safety inspectors use, with your evidence next to each item
  • Printouts with your legal name and license number, and an audit trail you can export

Product safety

Nothing unsafe leaves the building, and anything that does can be found fast.

  • A failed test blocks release and shipping. Returns and failures go on hold until QA decides
  • HACCP plans with critical control points, limits, corrective actions and a second person verifying
  • Line clearance before every run, and no clearance while equipment is out of service or past calibration
  • One step back to suppliers, one step forward to customers, and timed mock recalls
  • Approved chemicals with their SDS, approved suppliers, employee illness and hygiene checks

Standards

Built around the standards cannabis operators are audited against.

  • CSQ v2.0 for manufacturing, extraction and cultivation, clause by clause with evidence
  • cGMP and 21 CFR 117: hazard analysis, preventive controls, sanitation and records
  • HACCP plans built on its seven principles
  • Data you can trust: every record attributable, time-stamped and kept, with changes and the reason on file

Quality

A quality system that runs every day, not a binder that comes out before an audit.

  • Deviations and CAPA with root cause and an effectiveness check
  • Complaints, internal audits that show any clause not checked in 12 months, and management review
  • Risk register, change control, quality objectives with KPIs, and a verification schedule
  • Controlled SOPs with versions and read-and-sign, and training by role with quizzes
  • Approvals signed with the person's password, and two-step sign-in

Built for CSQ certification

CSQ (Cannabis Safety & Quality) is the accredited certification for licensed cannabis operations, audited by an independent certification body. Hendrix holds the CSQ v2.0 standard for your operation clause by clause, so every requirement has a way you meet it and the evidence to prove it.

The standard, clause by clause
Cultivation, extraction and manufacturing standards, with how you meet each clause, the evidence and the files, kept for each license.
Audits that cover everything
Internal audits record the clauses they checked. Hendrix shows any clause that hasn't been audited in 12 months, before the auditor does.
State rules first
CSQ expects you to meet your state's cannabis rules and show it. Add any state and its regulator; that state's rulebook sits next to the standard.
cGMP and food safety
HACCP plans with critical control points, monitoring and verification. The 21 CFR 117 checklist CFS inspectors use, hygiene training and the employee illness log.

What Hendrix keeps

Each license gets the pages it needs: production pages on manufacturing licenses, plant batches and capacity on cultivation licenses. Everyone sees only the licenses they work at.

Production
Batch records for extraction, pre-rolls, cartridges, gummies and infused flower. Manufacturing runs with material used, product made and waste. A planner and a live line map of every batch.
METRC and inventory
Receiving by METRC tag, a check of Hendrix counts against your METRC export, holds and release, shipping, and trace-forward for recalls.
Quality
Deviations and CAPA, complaints, internal audits, HACCP, risk register, change control and management review.
SOPs and training
Approved SOP versions, acknowledgements signed by each person, training courses with quizzes, and a training matrix by role.
Facility
Sanitation, pest, temperature and visitor logs on QR kiosks that keep working without internet. Time clock with badges, equipment and chemical records.
Cultivation
Plant batches from clone to harvest, room and bed capacity, then dry, cure and packaging.

Any state. Set up your way.

Hendrix isn't tied to one state's program. Add the states you're licensed in, and shape the records around your products and your SOPs.

Works in any state
Add a state with its regulator and its rulebook. Licenses in several states run side by side, each kept to its own rules, and METRC checks work wherever METRC is used.
Your products, your fields
Define your own SKUs and spec fields: infused or not and with what, coated or not and with what. Add your own materials, lists and log checklists.
Your people, your access
Build your own roles and decide, area by area and license by license, who can see, edit, approve and sign.
Your documents
Write SOPs in the SOP creator, build training courses and quizzes, and print everything with your legal names, license numbers and logo.

Ready when the inspector is

The records are built to be shown, not reconstructed the night before.

An audit trail nobody can edit
Every change is kept with who made it, when and why. Records are voided with a reason, never deleted.
A password at every signature
Approvals, releases and SOP acknowledgements are signed by the person, with their password, at the moment they sign.
METRC stays the source
Hendrix reads your METRC data to check counts and tags. It never writes to METRC.
Paperwork on demand
Batch records, training records and SOP packs, with or without signed acknowledgements, ready to print or save as PDF.
